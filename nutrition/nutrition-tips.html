Everyday Nutrition Tips

Learn simple habits to support a balanced lifestyle.

Healthy Habits

🥦 Eat Different Foods

Include a variety of vegetables, fruits, grains and protein foods in your meals.

💧 Drink Water

Drink water regularly throughout the day. Your needs may vary with activity and weather.

🥕 Eat Vegetables

Add vegetables to rice, sandwiches, pasta and other meals you enjoy.

🧼 Food Safety

Wash your hands and produce, and keep food stored safely.

🍽️ Eat Slowly

Take your time while eating and pay attention to your hunger and fullness.

🛌 Follow a Routine

Regular meals, sufficient sleep and physical activity support general wellbeing.
